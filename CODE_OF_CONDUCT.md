# Community expectations

Treat contributors with respect. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people; avoid harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing private information.

Use synthetic examples in issues and reviews. Do not upload information about identifiable students, teachers or schools. Separate measured engineering results from unverified educational claims.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict disruptive participation. If an incident involves private information, contact the maintainer through an available private contact channel on the GitHub profile rather than posting the details publicly.
